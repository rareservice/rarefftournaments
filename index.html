📜 Official Rules & Fair Play Policy

Strict competitive standards to guarantee a transparent, cheat-free esports experience.

🛡️

Anti-Cheat & Device Regulations

ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY
  • Mobile Only: Emulators (Bluestacks, LDPlayer, Gameloop, etc.) are strictly prohibited. Emulators will be kicked from the room without refund.
  • No Hacks or Configs: Any use of auto-aim, headshot configs, wallhacks, speed mods, or game-file manipulation will result in an immediate lifetime ban and forfeit of all wallet funds.
  • Teaming Up Prohibited: Collaborating with opponents in Solo or Duo matches is strictly forbidden. Disqualification is instant.
  • Device Checks: In high-stakes matches, tournament referees monitor live spectating feeds and game logs.
🎯

Custom Room Match Guidelines

FAIR PLAY STANDARDS
  • Punctuality: The custom room will launch exactly at the scheduled time. If you do not enter the room before kickoff, no refund will be issued.
  • Assigned Slot: You must sit in the slot number assigned to you in your 'My Matches' portal. Sitting in other players' slots will get you kicked by the referee.
  • Gun Attributes: In all competitive matches, Gun Attributes are turned OFF to ensure raw skill supremacy.
  • Character Skills: Character skills are enabled unless specifically designated as 'Skills OFF' in match details.
💵

Prize Payouts & Verification

GUARANTEED COIN CREDITS
  • Automatic Log Processing: Match kills and placements are cross-referenced with official referee scoreboards.
  • Screenshot Submission: Winners are requested to share a screenshot of the end-game victory screen via WhatsApp for express verification.
  • Credited in 15-30 Mins: Prize winnings are credited directly to your Withdrawable Winnings Balance within 30 minutes of match completion.
  • Instant Cashout: Withdraw anytime with 0% deduction to JazzCash, EasyPaisa, NayaPay, Binance Pay, or Any Crypto.
⚠️

Cancellation & Technical Issues

CONTINGENCY PROTOCOLS
  • Player Cancellation: You can cancel your tournament registration up to 30 minutes before match kickoff for a 100% instant refund.
  • Server Crash / Garena Glitch: In the rare event of a server disconnect or room crash, the match will be remade, or all entry fees will be refunded immediately.
  • Dispute Window: All disputes must be logged with support within 1 hour of match conclusion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my Custom Room ID & Password?

After registering for any tournament, go to the 'My Matches' tab. The Room ID and Password will automatically reveal 15 minutes prior to the start time with a 1-click copy button.

How are per-kill rewards calculated?

Each tournament specifies a per-kill reward. Even if your team does not win Booyah, every verified elimination made by your registered UID earns coins credited straight to your winnings balance.

How fast are wallet withdrawals processed?

Withdrawals to JazzCash, EasyPaisa, NayaPay, Binance Pay, and Any Crypto are processed within 15 to 30 minutes following tournament completion and result verification.

Can PC / Emulator players join?

No. To maintain fairness for competitive mobile athletes, all our official lobbies strictly enforce mobile-only play. Emulators are detected and blocked.
